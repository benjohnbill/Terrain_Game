# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The unaided-URL review the first version was built for **closed on 2026-08-05**.
The surface's current job is to carry a five-minute spoken presentation to an AI
business lab, one page section per beat, and to keep reading on its own once the
presentation is over. The product itself is intended for strategy players who
enjoy war simulations but avoid their long sessions, repetitive administration,
or familiar genre routines.

## Product Purpose

`Strategy Ground` presents a turn-based war strategy game in development. It
helps a first-time visitor understand that a small number of consequential
judgments about terrain, forces, information, and timing can accumulate into a
war-changing outcome. It now also has to expose why the game changed shape, what
market it is aimed at, and how it would earn money, because a business-lab
audience asks for those and the page is what they look at while hearing them.
It must still read without narration, because it outlives the talk.

## Positioning

A compressed war simulation where players shape the conditions of decisive
battles instead of managing a long queue of repetitive actions.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: open the playable development build, embedded and linked.
- Secondary CTA: none. The four-step war-model interaction that used to hold this
  slot is retired; a live build explains itself better than an explainer does.
- Ten-second memory: strategy is not the number of orders issued, but the
  consequences created by a few connected decisions.
- Belief ladder: this is a real game in development; its product premise is
  distinct; the current page separates implemented proof from future vision;
  the underlying systems can eventually support authored war stories.
- Proof on hand: a playable build that runs a full match, canonical design
  documents, a reproducible measurement of the design that was retired, and a
  six-title teardown of competitor product pages. No sales, reviews, awards, or
  release-date claims are available, and market demand is unvalidated.

## Brand Personality

Deliberate, consequential, and lucid. The voice is confident but not grandiose,
serious without imitating a military dashboard, and transparent about the
product's development state.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon command-center UI, emoji-led feature marketing, fake combat
telemetry, historical reenactment branding, and a template grid of
interchangeable feature cards. Do not imply that the current prototype is a
finished demo or that a Steam release date exists.

"Investor-pitch language" was on this list until 2026-08-05, when the
unaided-URL premise it protected expired. What it was guarding remains in force
and is stated directly instead: **no invented market figure, no revenue
projection, no unearned confidence about an undecided business model.** Stating a
business direction is allowed; stating it as a settled commitment is not, and the
page marks it as a direction under review.

## Design Principles

1. Show the chain from judgment to consequence instead of listing mechanics.
2. Let geography carry the visual identity without reducing the game to terrain.
3. Separate what works now from what the product aims to become.
4. Use the running build as evidence, not motion as decoration.
5. Make the product story understandable without narration, even though a
   narrator is now the first consumer.
6. One page section per spoken beat, in the order the story is told.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, semantic landmarks,
visible focus states, useful text alternatives, and a reduced-motion mode. The
page must remain understandable when animation or JavaScript is unavailable.
