# Capital — Glossary (Tier 1)

Birthplace definitions. Non-birthplace surfaces point here, never copy.

| Term | Definition | Summary | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| capital (수도) | A political designation the player places on ONE of the seat's main city sectors at match start. The polity layer's map anchor. In the 1v1 duel its fall is the SOLE win condition (ADR 0042); location is PUBLIC (fog covers guard strength only). Distinct from 主 도시 (main city), which is a map fact (authored population/economy spike, battle-summoning); the capital is a status ON such a sector. History: RULINGS CP-① (concept), CP-② (1v1 win-condition mechanics). |  | **AGREED** (concept 2026-07-10; 1v1 win-condition sealed 2026-07-23/24, CP-②; wiring stage ②) |
| capital guard (근위대) | The capital's standing guard, land-derived: coefficient (가안 **2,500**, CP-⑤ 2026-07-31; 구칭 가안 350, CP-①) × capital sector populationValue. Garrison-class — place-bound, own local ceiling outside fieldCap, register-backed; NO special supply rule (obeys `movement.isSupplied`, so it can be encircled and starved — the Moscow trap, CP-②). One stock with the final-battle capital garrison: wear at the front thins the last stand. Forward capitals gain emergent value from it (the guard holds the gate, freeing the field army). History: RULINGS CP-① (incl. rejected flat-1500 reuse and ×1.25 hub buff), CP-② (ordinary-garrison / encirclable seal), **CP-⑤ (coefficient re-cut for the duel — 350 left the guard weaker than one ordinary border shield)**. |  | **AGREED** (concept 2026-07-10; magnitude 가안, stage-② sweep) |
| capital fall (수도 함락) | The match-winning event: taking the enemy capital sector by defeating its capital guard. The SOLE win condition of the 1v1 duel (ADR 0042) — no points, no timeout-draw. Two late-game paths: overwhelming decisive battle, or Moscow-trap (encircle → cut supply → starve → finish). Early-rush is defended emergently (guard magnitude, encirclement visibility, mutual-exposure), never by a hard turn floor. History: RULINGS CP-② (D2.1/D2.3/D2.4). |  | **AGREED** (2026-07-23, CP-②) |
| forward / rear capital (전방 / 후방 수도) | The placement choice for the capital, an axis of leverage vs variance. Forward: guard holds the busiest gate for free → frees the field army for offense (天子守國門); short decapitation path, front wear thins the last stand = high leverage / high variance. Rear: pristine deep last stand (resilient / low variance); field army tied to gate defense, zero forward pressure. Emergent from the guard's duty-cycle, no multiplier. History: RULINGS CP-② (D1.4). |  | **AGREED** (2026-07-23, CP-②) |
| capital relocation (천도) | Moving the capital designation mid-match: possible but ruinous — consumes a large share of the commit budget (행동력) across ~2 turns. The guard is not stripped (place-bound); the cost is pure action-economy drain. The old seat stays win-target AND shield until relocation completes, then the target switches. Cost / turn count = 가안. History: RULINGS CP-② (D1.5). |  | **AGREED** (2026-07-23, CP-②; cost 가안) |
