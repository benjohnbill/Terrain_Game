# Fog-of-War & Discovery Glossary — Tier 1

Birthplace and single definition point of fog/perception vocabulary
(Vocabulary Law, `DOCUMENTATION-LAW.md`). Header format:
English canonical (한국어 표시어). Created 2026-07-10: the terminology
audit (Ring B, finding R-01) found this feature's load-bearing terms in
established use across DESIGN/ADRs/js with no registration surface.

| Term | Definition | Summary | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information confidence (정보 신뢰도) | How well the player knows a sector's mutable state — a **readout** of the grade and age of the observation testimony standing on it, reported on a scale from the public-bound floor to a ceiling short of certainty. It reports what the accumulated testimony already determines; it does not set the estimate band's width. Rises with contact or reconnaissance and erodes with elapsed time, per the snapshot-information principle (노화 헌법 P3). A separate layer OVER province status: status says what a province reads as, confidence says how far to trust that reading (ADR 0023 draws the distinction). **Re-cut 2026-08-03 (RULINGS ③)** — before that date this term named the *cause* of band width, and the reversed causation is stamped at ADR 0020. Code: `informationConfidence` in the reference archive (`js/intel.js` and consumers, ADR 0041); the L3 implementation arrives with build ticket 08. | How stale and how good the read is — a readout of standing testimony, not the cause of band width. | SEALED (re-cut 2026-08-03, RULINGS ③; registered 2026-07-10 from established usage) |
| Estimate band (추정 구간) | The banded display of a quantity the player cannot read exactly (enemy substance and fatigue, the reads derived from them, acceptance outlook, 판세): a range, never a false-precision point value. Its width comes from the **observation testimony** standing on the sector — how good the look was and how long ago it was taken — and is expressed as a fraction of the figure that testimony reported, **never of the true value**, so the band cannot be inverted to recover the truth. It always contains the truth and never collapses: an irreducible sliver survives any accumulation of testimony. The fog axis of the uncertainty duel — systems trip on true values while the player predicts through the band. **Re-cut 2026-08-03 (RULINGS ③)**; widths at `MAGNITUDE.md` FG-M①. | The true-containing range a player reads instead of a number; its width comes from testimony, never from the truth. | SEALED (re-cut 2026-08-03, RULINGS ③; registered 2026-07-10 from established usage) |
| Observation testimony (관측 증언) [조어] | A single act of observation, recorded as an honest but vague interval that **contains** the truth and stamped with the turn it was taken. Testimonies are what a viewer's projection is built from — the true value never enters it. They **accumulate** rather than replace: each is corrected forward to the present before the intersection is taken, using bounds derived from what could have changed since (recruitment rate, casualties, march reach), so an ageing testimony **widens** rather than becoming false. Free testimonies from battle contact are deliberately coarser than purchased reconnaissance. **What a testimony is *about* is its [[Testimony subject]]** (RULINGS ④, 2026-08-03), and accumulation runs only within [[Unbroken contact]] — across a gap in observation the Runtime does not link a new testimony to an older one. Coined 2026-08-03: the standard alternatives (contact report, sighting record) name the record without naming the property the whole model rests on — the witness is imprecise and never lies. Widths and prices: `MAGNITUDE.md` FG-M①. | One honest but vague look, timestamped — the unit a fog projection is built from. | SEALED (2026-08-03, RULINGS ③; scope of accumulation added 2026-08-03, RULINGS ④) |
| Testimony subject (증언 대상) | What one observation testimony is a statement **about**, and the thing its ageing bound must cover. Set by a single test: **can the subject move?** A field army's substance and fatigue can march away, so their testimony attaches to the **force**; a sector's garrison, 동원 강도 and civilian register cannot, so theirs attaches to the **sector**; 판세 attaches to the realm at match level. The distinction is why the same reconnaissance purchase yields readings that rot at different rates — a sector card holds its position while its figure blurs, a force marker holds its figure while its cone spreads. Not a choice between two models: both attachments are correct for their own observables, which is what RULINGS ④ found the recorded either/or had missed. The garrison row is conditional on garrisons staying immobile. A standard-English compound rather than a coinage, so it carries no `[조어]` tag. History and the ruling's reasons: `RULINGS.md` ④ decision 1. | Whether a testimony is about a force or a place — decided by whether the subject can move. | SEALED (2026-08-03, RULINGS ④) |
| Unbroken contact (연속 접촉) | The state of having observed a given force **every turn since** the first sighting, with no intervening turn unobserved. It is the only condition under which the Runtime links successive observations of the same force: while it holds, testimonies accumulate, the trend read builds, and a division of that force is seen as it happens; one unobserved turn cuts the chain, and a later sighting is a **new** contact the Runtime never joins to the old one. Because the sealed knowledge matrix carries no adjacency grade (Wayfinder gate 03 § 4), maintaining it is in practice paying the per-sector price again each turn — so it is what actually prices target tracking, and the price scales with the target's mobility because a faster force's one-turn reach cone spans more sectors to buy. Identity is therefore earned by observation rather than granted by the engine, which is what keeps free division from laundering an opponent's strength. A standard-English compound rather than a coinage, so it carries no `[조어]` tag. History and the ruling's reasons: `RULINGS.md` ④ decision 3. | Having watched a force every turn since first sighting — the only state in which the game links two looks as the same army. | SEALED (2026-08-03, RULINGS ④) |
