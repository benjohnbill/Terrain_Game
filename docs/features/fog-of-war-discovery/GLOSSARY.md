# Fog-of-War & Discovery Glossary — Tier 1

Birthplace and single definition point of fog/perception vocabulary
(Vocabulary Law, `DOCUMENTATION-LAW.md`). Header format:
English canonical (한국어 표시어). Created 2026-07-10: the terminology
audit (Ring B, finding R-01) found this feature's load-bearing terms in
established use across DESIGN/ADRs/js with no registration surface.

| Term | Definition | Summary | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information confidence (정보 신뢰도) | How well the player knows a sector's mutable state — a **readout** of the grade and age of the observation testimony standing on it, reported on a scale from the public-bound floor to a ceiling short of certainty. It reports what the accumulated testimony already determines; it does not set the estimate band's width. Rises with contact or reconnaissance and erodes with elapsed time, per the snapshot-information principle (노화 헌법 P3). A separate layer OVER province status: status says what a province reads as, confidence says how far to trust that reading (ADR 0023 draws the distinction). **Re-cut 2026-08-03 (RULINGS ③)** — before that date this term named the *cause* of band width, and the reversed causation is stamped at ADR 0020. Code: `informationConfidence` in the reference archive (`js/intel.js` and consumers, ADR 0041); the L3 implementation arrives with build ticket 08. | How stale and how good the read is — a readout of standing testimony, not the cause of band width. | SEALED (re-cut 2026-08-03, RULINGS ③; registered 2026-07-10 from established usage) |
| Estimate band (추정 구간) | The banded display of a quantity the player cannot read exactly (enemy substance and fatigue, the reads derived from them, acceptance outlook, 판세): a range, never a false-precision point value. Its width comes from the **observation testimony** standing on the sector — how good the look was and how long ago it was taken — and is expressed as a fraction of the figure that testimony reported, **never of the true value**, so the band cannot be inverted to recover the truth. It always contains the truth and never collapses: an irreducible sliver survives any accumulation of testimony. The fog axis of the uncertainty duel — systems trip on true values while the player predicts through the band. **Re-cut 2026-08-03 (RULINGS ③)**; widths at `MAGNITUDE.md` FG-M①. | The true-containing range a player reads instead of a number; its width comes from testimony, never from the truth. | SEALED (re-cut 2026-08-03, RULINGS ③; registered 2026-07-10 from established usage) |
| Observation testimony (관측 증언) [조어] | A single act of observation, recorded as an honest but vague interval that **contains** the truth and stamped with the turn it was taken. Testimonies are what a viewer's projection is built from — the true value never enters it. They **accumulate** rather than replace: each is corrected forward to the present before the intersection is taken, using bounds derived from what could have changed since (recruitment rate, casualties, march reach), so an ageing testimony **widens** rather than becoming false. Free testimonies from battle contact are deliberately coarser than purchased reconnaissance. Coined 2026-08-03: the standard alternatives (contact report, sighting record) name the record without naming the property the whole model rests on — the witness is imprecise and never lies. Widths and prices: `MAGNITUDE.md` FG-M①. | One honest but vague look, timestamped — the unit a fog projection is built from. | SEALED (2026-08-03, RULINGS ③) |
